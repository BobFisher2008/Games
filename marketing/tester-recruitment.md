# Tester recruitment messages

Placeholders to fill in once the closed test exists:
- `[test link]` / `[туршилтын холбоос]`: the opt-in link from Play Console → Testing → Closed testing
- `[form link]` / `[асуулгын холбоос]`: the feedback Google Form
- `[group link]`: the Google Group used as the tester list (Reddit exchanges)
- `[Play link]`: the Play Store link testers see after opting in

The "name in the credits" promise needs a credits screen in the game. Remove that line if we don't add one.
