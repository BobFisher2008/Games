
## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```text
Every roller paints a whole row or column. Every coat covers whatever is under it. Your job is to work out the order that turns a blank canvas into the picture on the reference card.

Last Coat is a calm logic puzzle built around one rule: the last coat wins. Find the line that went on last, then work backwards until the whole canvas makes sense.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a roller to paint its entire row or column.
• Match your canvas to the reference card.
• Order matters. A coat painted later covers the ones before it.
• Watch out for decoy rollers that would ruin the canvas.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 20 canvases that grow from 3×3 warm-ups to 6×6 brain-benders
• Endless mode with newly generated puzzles, every one solvable
• Three stars for a clean finish: no undos and no hints
• Paint cans: fill one by finishing canvases, then pick one of three rewards
• A daily gift with a 7-day streak
• Star rewards that unlock special frames and roller handles
• A studio of paint sets, roller handles and canvas frames to collect

PLAY YOUR WAY
• No timers and no lives. Undo whenever you like.
• Symbols mode marks every color with a shape for color-blind players.
• Works offline.
• Ads are optional. You only watch one when you choose to double a reward or get a free hint.

Short sessions, satisfying solves. Pick up a roller and find the last coat.
```
