Last Coat

Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy covers Last Coat: Paint Puzzle ("Last Coat"), a puzzle game for Android made by an independent developer.

The short version

  • Last Coat doesn't ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. It isn't sent to the developer.
  • The developer doesn't receive, sell or share any personal data about you.
  • If the game shows ads, they come from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show them. See Ads.

What the game stores on your phone

Last Coat saves your progress so you can pick up where you left off: finished canvases and stars, paint drops, items you've collected, your daily gift streak, and your settings (sound, vibration and symbols mode).

This data is kept in the app's own storage on your phone. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own Google account backup. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

No analytics or tracking

Last Coat doesn't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools. It doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Ads

Last Coat may show optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. You only see an ad when you tap a button to watch one, for example to double a reward or get a free hint.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and how you interact with ads. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's Google settings, under Ads.

Children

Last Coat isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can turn sound and vibration off in the game's Studio. You can delete all game data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Because the developer doesn't hold any data about you, there's nothing else to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the game, email the developer address shown on Last Coat's Google Play page, or open an issue at github.com/BobFisher2008/Games/issues.
